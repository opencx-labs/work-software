Use the mobile web client with your desktop or a shared server. There is no separate App Store download.

Desktop pairing needs a reachable desktop on your local network. For access away from your desk, connect to a reachable Catamorphic server.

  1. Open your workspace.

    Start the desktop app and open the project you want to continue.

  2. Choose “Continue on mobile”.

    Find the command in the desktop command palette, then scan the QR code with your phone.

  3. Keep the conversation going.

    Open the link and follow the connection steps. You can also connect through your team's server invitation.

Before you head out.

Does my computer need to stay on?

If you connect directly to the desktop, it must stay on and reachable. A shared server gives you a separate place to run the work and needs to be reachable from your phone.

Can I use it away from the office?

Yes, when you connect to a Catamorphic server reachable from your phone. Pairing directly to your desktop is a local-network connection, not automatic remote access.

Is it a separate mobile app?

It is a mobile web app that runs in your phone's browser. It connects to your desktop or server; it is not a standalone AI service or an App Store download.
