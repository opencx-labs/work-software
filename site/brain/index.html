Your questions, answered.

Is this a hosted service?

Today, the company brain runs on your own infrastructure using the Catamorphic server. This website does not provide a managed hosting signup. The setup guide covers the supported deployment paths.

Does everyone need technical skills?

No. Server setup needs technical help, but teammates use projects and conversations through the desktop or mobile web client. A company brain can support customer work, operations, research, and other everyday work.

How does it learn about our company?

From the knowledge you bring into its projects, the instructions you give its assistants, and the tools you connect. It does not automatically gain access to your company's private systems.
